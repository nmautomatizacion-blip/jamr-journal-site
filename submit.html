Submission Guidelines

Manuscripts should be prepared using the JAMR LaTeX template and submitted via e-mail to the editorial office. The article processing charge (APC) for accepted manuscripts is USD 1,450, payable in standard fiat or in ETH/USDC.

Please address submissions to editor@jamr-journal.online. Allow up to 21 days for a first decision.
