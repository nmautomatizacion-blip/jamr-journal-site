About the Journal

The Journal of Applied Matter Reviews (JAMR) is an open-access, peer-reviewed journal covering original research in materials science, nanocomposites, metallurgy, and applied physics. JAMR was founded in 2013 and publishes quarterly, with an annual volume of approximately 180 articles.

The journal operates under a single-blind peer review model, with average time-to-first-decision of 21 days. All accepted articles are distributed under CC-BY-NC 4.0. Article processing charges are listed in the Submit section.

JAMR is published by the JAMR Editorial Office, with administrative support by Aletheia Scholarly Services Ltd. (Nicosia, Cyprus) for copy-editing, typesetting, and submission logistics.
